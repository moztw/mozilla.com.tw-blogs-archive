Firefox 19 版已將 PDF.js [內建為 PDF 檢視器](https://website-archive.mozilla.org/www.mozilla.org/firefox_releasenotes/en-US/firefox/19.0/releasenotes/)，只要上網遇到 PDF 文件即可[直接在瀏覽器中閱讀](https://blog.mozilla.org/blog/2013/02/19/firefox-introduces-pdf-viewer-to-browse-the-web-without-interruption/)而不用中斷上網。這種使用經驗真正實踐了網路即是平台的精神。

[![](https://images-blogger-opensocial.googleusercontent.com/gadgets/proxy?url=http%3A%2F%2F4.bp.blogspot.com%2F-bg1rCEqSqhY%2FVCDho1Zmy6I%2FAAAAAAAAVBo%2FDNOnyRxg2p4%2Fs1600%2F2014-09-23%252B10%253A57%253A16%252B%25E7%259A%2584%25E8%259E%25A2%25E5%25B9%2595%25E6%2593%25B7%25E5%259C%2596.png&container=blogger&gadget=a&rewriteMime=image%2F*)](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-bg1rCEqSqhY/VCDho1Zmy6I/AAAAAAAAVBo/DNOnyRxg2p4/s1600/2014-09-23%2B10%3A57%3A16%2B%E7%9A%84%E8%9E%A2%E5%B9%95%E6%93%B7%E5%9C%96.png)

在大多數的情況下，使用 Firefox 做為 PDF 檢視器很是方便，且有些少見格式的檔案仍然可以用本機的其他檢視器打開。不過遇上編輯或是列印的時候，內建的檢視器就顯得稍為不夠用了。要如何因應這個情形呢？

我自己有兩種方法。首先就是繼續使用預設的行為，但是遇到有進一步需求的檔案，我會選擇按右上角的「下載」以其他程式再處理。

[![](https://images-blogger-opensocial.googleusercontent.com/gadgets/proxy?url=http%3A%2F%2F1.bp.blogspot.com%2F-6qz9pzOjiYA%2FVCDlmIWMyPI%2FAAAAAAAAVB4%2FTHRayUummjo%2Fs1600%2F%25E7%2581%25AB%25E7%258B%2590%25E6%2588%25AA%25E5%259C%2596_2014-09-23T03-13-21.681Z.png&container=blogger&gadget=a&rewriteMime=image%2F*)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-6qz9pzOjiYA/VCDlmIWMyPI/AAAAAAAAVB4/THRayUummjo/s1600/%E7%81%AB%E7%8B%90%E6%88%AA%E5%9C%96_2014-09-23T03-13-21.681Z.png)

如果想要跳過預設的檢視器呢？其實很簡單，只要從 Firefox 的「工具」→「選項」→「應用程式」中找到「PDF」，確認其預設的行為是「在 Firefox 中預覽」即可。

[![](https://images-blogger-opensocial.googleusercontent.com/gadgets/proxy?url=http%3A%2F%2F1.bp.blogspot.com%2F-HKqkC-grY0g%2FVCDkINscyhI%2FAAAAAAAAVBw%2F7UY4ry1kNcg%2Fs1600%2F2014-09-23%252B10%253A29%253A31%252B%25E7%259A%2584%25E8%259E%25A2%25E5%25B9%2595%25E6%2593%25B7%25E5%259C%2596.png&container=blogger&gadget=a&rewriteMime=image%2F*)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-HKqkC-grY0g/VCDkINscyhI/AAAAAAAAVBw/7UY4ry1kNcg/s1600/2014-09-23%2B10%3A29%3A31%2B%E7%9A%84%E8%9E%A2%E5%B9%95%E6%93%B7%E5%9C%96.png)

如果每次遇到 PDF 檔案都要詢問你想採取的行為，就改成「每次詢問」吧。或是有其他比較常用的外掛程式也可以在這邊選擇。

[![](https://images-blogger-opensocial.googleusercontent.com/gadgets/proxy?url=http%3A%2F%2F2.bp.blogspot.com%2F-OoD4AGlviD0%2FVCDl4IBt-9I%2FAAAAAAAAVCA%2FNtB19JkDoRM%2Fs1600%2F2014-09-23%252B10%253A30%253A18%252B%25E7%259A%2584%25E8%259E%25A2%25E5%25B9%2595%25E6%2593%25B7%25E5%259C%2596.png&container=blogger&gadget=a&rewriteMime=image%2F*)](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-OoD4AGlviD0/VCDl4IBt-9I/AAAAAAAAVCA/NtB19JkDoRM/s1600/2014-09-23%2B10%3A30%3A18%2B%E7%9A%84%E8%9E%A2%E5%B9%95%E6%93%B7%E5%9C%96.png)

之後再遇到 PDF 檔案就會出現這個對話框囉。
